🚦 Before You Submit Checklist

Use this checklist before submitting discussions, short answers, essays, projects, or presentations.

🧠 Concepts: Did I use important course concepts?
📖 Explanation: Did I explain the concepts in my own words?
🔍 Examples: Did I give examples or evidence?
🎯 Connection: Did I connect my answer back to the prompt?
🔄 Application: Did I show how the concept works in the situation?
